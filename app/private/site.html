What we do

A Nairobi-based advisory that helps international firms win here.

Tender Bridge Africa Ventures helps international construction and infrastructure firms — companies with no presence in Kenya — enter and win government and donor-funded tenders across roads, energy, water and health infrastructure. We take you from a single opportunity to a submitted, competitive bid: finding the right tenders, meeting the eligibility and registration requirements, setting up a credible local partner, and preparing bids to the standard these contracts demand.

Foreign firms rarely lose these tenders on capability. They lose them on access.

A World Bank or government infrastructure tender in Kenya is open to international firms — but winning one means clearing a specific path first: prequalification, local registrations, a credible partner on the ground, bonds, and bids prepared to the procurement rules that eliminate unprepared entrants.

Tender Bridge Africa Ventures closes that gap. We are a Nairobi-based advisory that takes an international contractor from "interested" to "shortlisted and bidding" — handling the eligibility, the compliance and the local partnership so your firm competes on what it does best.

Opportunity intelligence

Curated, analysed tenders — not raw lists. Each comes with the buyer, the eligibility read, and an honest view of how a foreign firm would win it.

Eligibility & registration

NCA and KRA registration, World Bank and UNGM supplier databases, and prequalification — the paperwork required before you can bid, handled for you.

Local partnership & presence

A credible local partner or joint venture, and representation on the ground — the presence these tenders expect, without you first opening an office.

Bid preparation

World Bank-compliant documentation, securities and bonds, and the documentation discipline these tenders demand — prepared with you, end to end.
